VuiAdmin is the kind of admin theme that usually gets sold. We keep it MIT, and sponsors are what
make that possible.

## What your money actually pays for

Six framework editions of the same nineteen screens is more work than it sounds. A card has to be the
same card in React as it is in Blade. Dark mode has to invert properly rather than wash out. Every
control needs keyboard and screen-reader behaviour. Every edition needs the parity checks that stop
them quietly drifting apart, and each framework ships a major version every few months.

We do that so you do not have to build it or buy it.

## Even $1 a month helps

It goes toward bug fixes, new screens, and keeping the demos and the docs current. Honestly, it is
what keeps us building in the open rather than putting this behind a licence.

If VuiAdmin saved you a fortnight of work, and it is designed to, a sponsorship is the best
thank-you there is.

## Other ways to help, all free

- **Star the repository** for the edition you use. It is the signal that decides whether anyone else
  finds this.
- **Report a bug** with the steps. A screen that renders wrong on your browser is a screen we cannot
  see.
- **Tell someone.** Most people paying for an admin theme do not know a free one at this standard
  exists.
